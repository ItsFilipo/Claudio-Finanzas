Scope: index.html, the whole app (single screen app with tabs). Mode: Operate.
Audience/job: one person in Colombia, on the phone, logs an expense in 5-10 s right after paying (COP).
Direction chosen by user: "Sobres de presupuesto" (cash-envelope method). Roll ran degraded (no challengers).

## Direction contract
THESIS: Every category is a cash envelope you pay out of; budgets are money inside the envelope, not a table. Refuses the bank-app dashboard of charts and cards.
OWN-WORLD: Pale security-paper green ground (#EDF0EA / dark #0F1411), deep peso-green ink and accent (#16603F). Envelopes tinted in Colombian banknote hues (ocre, azul, morado, rojo, naranja, verde) with a V flap and a round seal holding the icon. Money left fills the envelope from the bottom. Bricolage Grotesque for amounts, Manrope for UI.
STORY: Open, see how much is left this month and per envelope, tap "Anotar gasto", type the amount, tap the envelope it came from: saved.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Month switcher at top; one large "te quedan" figure; 2-column envelope grid; fixed bottom tab bar with a centered green "Anotar gasto" pill.
FORM: grounded list position 1 (cash envelopes), chosen by user over assigned candidate 4 (squared notebook); seed key d81008ba.
Signature interaction: after saving, the chosen envelope's flap lifts and its remaining amount counts down while its fill drops.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
